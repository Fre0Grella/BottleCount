# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small party hosts and organizers planning bar inventory and tickets, often on a laptop but needing the same flow to work smoothly on mobile. Door staff use mobile to scan tickets quickly and reliably.

## Product Purpose

Help small hosts plan barback inventory quantities and manage tickets easily without feeling overwhelmed by complex, professional-grade tools.

## Brand Personality

Precise, professional, intuitive.

## Anti-references

Neon nightlife apps, overly playful party apps, and rigid accounting-style dashboards.

## Design Principles

1. Reduce overwhelm through progressive disclosure and clear priorities.
2. Make the most important outcomes visible at a glance.
3. Maintain trust with transparent, offline-first behavior.
4. Support fast switching between planning and event operations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA, reduced motion support, and strong contrast for both dark and light themes.
